Tisa Product ZIP Importer

Installation:
1. Upload the generated plugin ZIP through WordPress Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin.
2. Activate it from WordPress Plugins.
3. Make sure WooCommerce is active.
4. Go to WooCommerce > ورود محصول تیساکیس.
5. Upload product.zip generated by the Telegram bot.

Resource and safety limits (version 0.8.0):
- ZIP upload: maximum 32 MiB compressed, 128 MiB expanded, and 200 total entries.
- Images: maximum 40 images per archive and 12 MiB per image.
- product.json: exactly one file, maximum 2 MiB. Unsafe paths, symlinks, duplicate paths, and extreme compression ratios are rejected before extraction.
- ZIP staging uses a random permission-restricted directory outside WordPress, the document root, and the uploads directory; importing fails closed if no such temporary directory is available. A scheduled cleanup removes abandoned staging data.
- A product may have at most 500 variations. The Cartesian count is calculated before product creation or any existing-product mutation.
- Large new-product imports (>20 variations, >5 images, >16 MiB expanded data, or >40 ZIP entries) use Action Scheduler batches of at most 20 variations and 3 images (WP-Cron fallback when Action Scheduler is unavailable). The new product remains a draft while its background job runs; refresh the importer page to view status.
- Existing-product restocks are preflighted against the 500-variation cap and the existing variation count is limited to 500; variation combinations are generated by index rather than keeping a full Cartesian matrix in memory.
- Next SKU is calculated from WooCommerce's indexed product-meta lookup table and exact SKU lookups, not by loading all _sku postmeta into PHP.

The importer creates draft products, leaves the description empty, and uses one SKU on the parent product. Variation records do not receive separate SKUs.
